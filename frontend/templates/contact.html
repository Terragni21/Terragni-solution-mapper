Good to know

Questions people ask before they reach out.

Do I need a clearly defined problem?

No. Many conversations start with "something is not moving". Terragni starts with sensemaking: understanding the challenge beneath the challenge.

What happens to the details I share?

They are used only so a Terragni consultant can contact you about your enquiry. Please do not share client names, account numbers or personal customer data.

Which industries does Terragni work in?

Manufacturing, financial services, insurance, healthcare, consumer, retail and emerging businesses. The Solution Mapper prototype focuses on banking.

Can I try the Solution Mapper first?

Yes. Describe a challenge or ask a question about Terragni on the Solution Mapper, and the page you get is shared with us when you contact us.
