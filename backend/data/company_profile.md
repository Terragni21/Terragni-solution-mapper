## Who Terragni is
Terragni Consulting (P) Ltd. is a behavioural strategy and human engagement consulting firm based in Pune, India. Its tagline is "the Human Engagement company". Terragni helps organisations understand the human behaviour behind business challenges and create the conditions for meaningful movement. Its guiding line is: "When behaviour shifts, business moves."

## What Terragni believes
Businesses do not change when strategy changes. They change when behaviour changes. Strategy creates intent; behaviour creates movement. Many organisations do not have a strategy problem, they have a behaviour problem: customers are not acting, employees are not adopting, teams are not changing, or transformation is not translating into behaviour. Terragni looks beyond the visible problem to the human dynamics underneath it.

## The Terragni lens: Friction, Motivation, Context (FMC)
Behaviour happens at the intersection of three forces. Friction is what gets in the way. Motivation is what drives people. Context is what shapes behaviour. Terragni's approach is: find the friction, understand the motivation, reshape the context.

## How Terragni works
Terragni does not start with solutions; it starts with sensemaking. Its five-step way of working is: 1) Sensemaking — understand the challenge beneath the challenge; 2) Diagnose — identify the behavioural forces at play; 3) Decode — understand what people think, feel and do; 4) Design — create interventions around real behavioural dynamics; 5) Deliver — translate insight into measurable movement.

## Toolkit and disciplines
Terragni combines several disciplines to understand behaviour: behavioural science, neuroscience, ethnography, data and design thinking.

## What Terragni helps move
Customers (engagement, conversion, trust, experience and action), employees (engagement, adoption, productivity and change) and channels (behaviour across the systems, moments and environments where people act).

## Main services (offerings)
- De-Sludge: removes internal friction ("sludge") in processes, culture, capability and technology that stops an organisation from being truly customer-centric.
- EAS (Effort Assessment Score): measures and reduces customer effort across time, physical, cognitive and emotional dimensions at every touchpoint of a journey.
- Experiential Segmentation: segments customers by the experience they seek, not by demographics, so engagement, service and communication can be tailored.
- JUMP: behavioural design for digital adoption and engagement; fixes friction in digital, app and web journeys. JUMP Probe is a rapid audit of a digital journey.
- ME (Marketing Effectiveness): uses the Friction x Motivation x Context model and behavioural nudges to improve positioning, communication and retention.
Other Terragni platforms include LENS (insights as a service), Terragni Sensemaking, TruNorth and customer engagement solutions.

## Industries Terragni works in
Manufacturing, financial services (including banking), insurance, healthcare, consumer, retail and emerging businesses. Different industries, similar human dynamics. The Solution Mapper prototype currently focuses on banking.

## Business impact
Terragni measures its work by what changes in the real world. Reported impact: about ₹120 crore of revenue unlocked, about ₹50 crore of avoidable cost removed, and more than 15 years of experience.

## The name Terragni
The name joins two ideas: Terra, the earth, representing grounded reality, and Agni, the fire, representing transformative energy. Terra + Agni = Terragni: grounded in reality, driven by transformation.

## Brand colours, logo and font
Terragni's main brand colour is purple, used with a warm gold/yellow accent. The logo is a yellow "T" mark with "TERRAGNI CONSULTING" in purple and the line "the Human Engagement company". Purple was chosen because it is where different worlds come together: it reflects synthesis — bringing different perspectives, disciplines and ways of seeing together — and stands for contrast, possibility and transformation. Terragni's brand font is Montserrat.

## Thinking and publications
Terragni publishes The SHIFT, a newsletter of original research, industry studies, synthesised insights and long reads: https://terragni.in/newsletter-the-shift/ . Terragni also shares behavioural insights and the Terragni perspective on what needs to move in organisations.
