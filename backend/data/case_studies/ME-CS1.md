**Client**: India's 2nd largest private bank — 1,600 branches, 1.5M customers pan-India.

**Challenge**: Increasing wallet share and word-of-mouth of the affluent program; driving
customer engagement and revenue through use of services.

**Key finding**: The affluent program delivers hassle-free banking and the "Indian Brand" is a
strength that can be leveraged more strongly for emotional engagement. But experientially the
program under-delivers on Identity & Social Dimensions; the Relationship Manager is not
effective in support or service recovery; a breakdown in friction-free transactions disengages
customers from the whole program; and awareness of program benefits is very low — benefits
aren't perceived as relevant.

**Solution — Eliminating perceived friction via behaviour-science-led communication**:
Communication redesigned to provide clarity and evoke "the future state" for each segment,
using behaviour-science principles to design artefacts that communicate exclusivity,
personalised prestige, convenience and aspiration (e.g. a redesigned premium credit-card
comms piece: "A League Apart" — dedicated relationship team, digital payment solutions,
customised cards & investment offers, curated events, clear eligibility criteria).

**Business outcome**: Increased wallet share, increased customer acquisition through
recommendation, increased customer retention.
